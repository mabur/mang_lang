STACK FOR FAST NAME LOOKUP

* Store all name definitions
* Similar to constructing evaluated dictionaries
* Create copies of expressions on the stack for now. Later on we could just store them on the stack.
* Each item on the stack is an Expression, corresponding to the right hand side of a definition.
* Let the dictionary construction handle resizing of the stack when dictionaries goes in and out of scope.
* There is an issue with closures, that can be solved with up-values.
{
  a = 1
  f = in x out result@{b=x result=a}
  c = f!5
  d = f!6
}
